# EQUIS BRAND SYSTEM — PUBLIC WEBSITE

## Brand essence
EQUIS is a premium nutrition and training brand built around discipline, practical routines and individualized progress. It helps people work toward body-shape goals through realistic nutrition and gym-aligned lifestyle support.

## Audience
- Egypt and Gulf countries.
- Busy professionals.
- Gym-focused clients.
- People pursuing fat loss, muscle gain or body recomposition.

## Visual direction
Quiet, premium, structured, editorial and human. The site should feel calm and confident, not aggressive or clinical. Use whitespace, clear hierarchy, restrained color and strong typography.

## Approved color tokens
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Evergreen | `#183F35` | Primary brand, headings, navigation, CTA surfaces |
| Warm Cream | `#F6F1E7` | Main background and reversed surfaces |
| Clay | `#A9684A` | Small accent only |
| Sage | `#9CA995` | Secondary panels and quiet support |
| Warm Neutral | `#D9D2C6` | Borders and dividers |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and content surfaces |

Evergreen on cream is the main contrast pair. Check UI combinations for accessible contrast.

## Typography
- UI/body: Manrope (400, 500, 600, 700).
- Editorial headings: Newsreader (400, 500).
- Logo: supplied artwork only. Never recreate the logo with typed text.
- Arabic fallback: Noto Sans Arabic or another legible licensed Arabic family. Test on real devices.

## Logo assets
Use:
- `public/brand/EQUIS_NUTRITION_Symbol.png`
- `public/brand/EQUIS_NUTRITION_Wordmark.png`

Preserve aspect ratio and clear space. Do not distort, recolor, crop into the artwork, add effects or separate the wordmark from its approved relationship. Use standalone symbol only for compact contexts and the full wordmark where space allows.

## Layout
- Desktop content max width: approximately 1200px.
- Comfortable section spacing; avoid dense text walls.
- Rounded cards restrained (8–14px), subtle borders, no heavy shadows.
- Mobile first; no horizontal overflow in Arabic RTL.
- Use authentic, licensed photography.
- Do not use AI-generated fake client transformations, fake testimonial portraits or misleading before/after imagery.
- Food photography should feel natural, clean and appetizing.

## Voice
Direct, supportive, disciplined and realistic.
Avoid shame, fear, unrealistic timelines, miracle claims and body-negative language.
Emphasize consistency and individualized coaching rather than shortcuts.

## Content restrictions
Never invent testimonials, reviews, client transformations, prices, guarantees, clinical claims, founder credentials or contact information.
Keep unavailable content disabled or marked as draft.
