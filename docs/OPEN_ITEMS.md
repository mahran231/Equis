# OPEN ITEMS — BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH

Build around editable placeholders. Do not invent these details.

- Official domain.
- Public contact email.
- WhatsApp/phone number and preferred CTA.
- Official Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and other social URLs.
- Final approved founder biography and credentials wording.
- Confirmed service scope and whether services are online, in-person or both.
- Prices, only if the owner wants them published.
- Approved FAQ answers.
- Approved testimonials and exact publication permissions.
- Approved transformation photos and exact publication permissions.
- Approved EQUIS Recipes/resources content.
- Privacy Policy and Terms reviewed for Egypt and target Gulf markets.
- Contact-form delivery provider and sender identity.
- Analytics choice, if any, subject to privacy review.

These are content/configuration requirements, not reasons to build a coaching portal.
