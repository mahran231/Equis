This package does not include the original EQUIS logo, private client masters, exercise library, recipe book or running application source. Do not pretend otherwise. No existing app repository is required for a from-scratch implementation. Preparation can continue with clearly labeled neutral/synthetic fixtures; final branding/content and launch cannot pass without real approved assets.

## Owner decisions, ask together
1. **Surface launch:** confirm public marketing website AND private coach/client webapp launch together, or stage separately. Both are specified here; this is not a hidden choice to omit one.
2. **Visual sign-off:** final app palette, typography and layout from identity. Current website color experiments are not app approval. Preview tokenized screens first. Confirm exact current PDF credentials/footer because older PDF wording and later public title differ.
3. **Commercial rules:** current installments, Elite review wording, freeze allowance conversion (calendar month or fixed days), start/end month semantics, cancellation/upgrade terms, payment instructions and support-window interpretation. No payment collection is built by default.
4. **Access:** verified owner identity, authorized coaches/partner, whether historical conversations transfer when a client changes coach, adults-only launch or approved minors workflow. Do not create a partner account on a guessed identity.
5. **Privacy/operations:** health-record retention/deletion rules, consent/privacy contact, hosting/data region review, backup destination/recovery targets, incident owner and budget. 30-day lead cleanup does not answer health-record retention.
6. **Communication/content:** reminder defaults/quiet hours, support expectations, approved check-in fields/cadence, required English/French coverage, exact book fulfillment mode. Current default is manual WhatsApp book delivery with no automatic send.
7. **Adherence policy:** accept/modify formula proposed in technical spec, partial logs, past-plan revision treatment and late corrections. Until approved, show counts and N/A rather than a misleading percentage.
8. **Go-live:** explicit approval for chosen host/provider costs, domain/DNS changes, production deployment and real client data. No prior app pause is silently reopened by delivering this package.

Technical choices belong to developer; do not ask the owner to select frameworks or RLS syntax. Ask only unresolved business/access/cost decisions. Document answer, date and impacted requirement. Continue independent development while narrow gates remain open.
